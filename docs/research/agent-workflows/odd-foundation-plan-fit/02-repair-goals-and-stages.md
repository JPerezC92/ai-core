## Repair goals (plan, Repair goals R1–R4; brief Q4)

- **R1 — preserve lifecycle and draft safety: weak/partial — essentially a non-fit.** No ODD mechanism addresses ignore-blocker safety or audit-gate enforcement specifically. Two thin transfers (`infer:`): "effective-ignore-or-stop without skill-owned ignore edits" (plan, R1) echoes read-only-first plus "findings do not silently expand authorization" (S4, S5); "enforce the existing audit contract once through common pure validation" (plan, R1) echoes the enforcement maxim at S7. Both are philosophical backing only — **downgrade: cosmetic if presented as a mechanism**.
- **R2 — coherent instructions; snapshot-bound review: strong.** "count failed correction rounds within a task while binding each review to its snapshot" (plan, R2, How) is RDD's core: the receipt is "bound to the exact bytes reviewed — not to the branch, not to the PR — recomputed against real git state at each lifecycle point" (S8); "The native review candidate is a work-unit commit or a PR slice, never a TODO checkbox and never the accumulated feature branch" (S4). This matches the plan's snapshot-bound reviews (plan, Evidence inspected and limits, `clean-architecture-contract` AC10–14 row) and Stage E ("stabilize reviewed inputs", "reuse only unchanged evidence"). Partial elements: RDD bounds correction to **one** round (S6) while the plan allows two before reassessment (plan, Verification, Stop) — RDD is stricter, not identical (see [`README.md`](README.md) gap 4); and RDD's receipts are tool-recomputed hashes while the plan binds reviews to recorded SHA256 candidates (plan, Foundation planning review "Reviewed inputs: Plan SHA256…"). `infer:` the practical delta is to generalize an existing practice, not import a tool.
- **R3 — contract and rollout accounting: partial.** "record actual approvals and stage evidence, and distinguish pending history from delivery" (plan, R3, How) resonates with `## Log` discipline — `L1` is "your original request verbatim" with later "corrections, evidence, and rationale" appended (S4) — and with "A requirement change rewrites only the affected spec and reopens only its task" (S4), which matches "do not append new promises over contradictory old ones or reset unrelated marks" (plan, Existing-story policy). `infer:` the fit is real but structural; ODD has no approval/supersession-record concept and no pending-vs-delivered distinction — the plan's own records (e.g. "That 'no source repair has started' clause is historical", plan, Current preparation state) are more precise than ODD's log.
- **R4 — exercise rather than describe: strong.** ODD's close triad and failure-reproduction rule (S4) plus the reproducibility standard "A number I publish and you cannot reproduce is a claim about my honesty" (S7) map directly onto R4's "record compact reproducible inputs/deltas, expected/observed decisions and actual executors" (plan, Execution contracts 6) and "unavailable timings or comparable historical measurements remain explicitly unmeasured" (plan, R4, Done when). `infer:` S7 is the strongest single external statement of R4's epistemics in the whole evidence set.

## Stages A–G (plan, Complete incremental rollout; brief Q5 — one bounded paragraph each)

- **A — Foundation and placement: partial.** ODD's entry/routing model and the one-home rule (S4) support G1's structural outcome (see [`01-goal-mapping.md`](01-goal-mapping.md)), and "Record a preparation/review baseline without promising a speedup" (plan, Stage A) is consistent with the ecosystem's own self-critical disclosure posture (the author publishes the ~3× slowdown on large tasks alongside favorable numbers, S9, S28 — self-run, n=1, directional only; no speedup claim is made or implied here). `infer:` the mechanism gap stands: ODD's default-on behavior is delivered by a harness binary, not by prose; and under ODD's own resumability test much of increment A would classify "small" and receive no durable task artifacts (S4, S5), while the plan runs it under a full plan and audit discipline. Partial, with a threshold divergence.
- **B — Self-improvement authoring: partial.** ODD's handoff rule ("never a paraphrase… read until `## Log`", S4) supports Stage B's "explicit support-file reads", and the ecosystem's preset skills (e.g. `skill-creator`) plus community skill library (S4, S25) show authoring artifacts as first-class governed objects. `infer:` the strongest B-relevant lesson is cautionary, not mechanistic: the ecosystem's own version sprawl made "pre-v4 'SDD workflow' material already stale" (S11; 05 §5 risks) — concrete evidence for B's mission that "future instructions do not reproduce bloat or contradictory authority" (plan, Stage B). No ODD mechanism enforces bounded authored content; that remains the plan's Small-document budgets.
- **C — Story workflow and existing contracts: partial + one conflict.** Strong sub-mapping: "A requirement change rewrites only the affected spec and reopens only its task" (S4) matches "do not append new promises over contradictory old ones or reset unrelated marks" (plan, Existing-story policy) and R3's supersession labeling. Conflicting sub-mapping: ODD's single living document mixes `## Specs`/`## Tasks`/`## Log` (S4) while Stage C requires "separate promises, constraints, Proof and execution history" (plan, Stage C) — see [`03-conflicts-and-guardrails.md`](03-conflicts-and-guardrails.md), conflict 7. `infer:` ODD contributes spec-hygiene heuristics (verbatim quoting of "your exact strings, error messages, and examples" (S4) is directly useful for story criteria precision) but no catalogue/collision concept; full-catalogue review (plan, Evidence inspected row `user-story`) has no ODD counterpart.
- **D — Plan preparation: strongest intent match, one method clash.** ODD's classification is "by resumability, not size": small when "understood, risk is contained, and the work could be resumed from the request plus `git diff`"; "Counts of files, commands, tests, fixes, or a requested todo list never decide it" (S4) — this is exactly the shape of Stage D's "consistent lightweight eligibility" and "contract-sized phases instead of private coding recipes" (plan, Stage D). The method clash: the ~400-authored-line figure is "only a planning heuristic, not a task acceptance criterion, hard cap, counter-trigger, automatic stop, forced split, or RDD trigger" (S4), while the plan's Small-document budgets are "enforceable first-increment delivery limits" with hard line **and** word maxima (plan, Small-document budgets). `infer:` and explicitly **downgraded** — the analogy is partially cosmetic: ODD's 400 lines govern *authored changed lines per work unit*; the plan's budgets govern *document size*. The two numbers regulate different objects, so "ODD supports soft budgets" and "ODD opposes hard budgets" are both overstatements (see [`README.md`](README.md) gap 3). What transfers cleanly is the eligibility criterion (resumability), not the budget philosophy. Note also ODD's work-unit slicing ("reviewable slices of about 400 authored changed lines… a unit whose Risk line names a high-risk item gets its own commit", S4) does echo "contract-sized phases" as reviewability-driven sizing.
- **E — Implementation and review reliability: strong.** RDD supplies the densest mechanism set: byte-frozen receipts (S8, S6), risk-frozen review depth (low → silent structural checks; medium → 1 focus lens + consent; high → canonical 4R + consent + forecast) (S6), one bounded correction (S6), evidence classes — findings require `evidence_class`, `causal_disposition`, and concrete changed-hunk/differential-test/before-after proof (S20) — and per-commit/PR-slice candidates ("high → the commit itself is the candidate, medium → defers to the PR slice", S4, S13). These map onto "stabilize reviewed inputs; consolidate findings and corrections; reuse only unchanged evidence" (plan, Stage E) and R2. Also "A message may name a command only if running that command resolves the block" (S7) matches the plan's "exact invocation evidence" (plan, Execution contracts 6). `infer:` strong; caveat that risk-frozen depth would collide with this repo's fixed gate types if imported literally (Sentinel 🛡️ (Quality Guardian) / Bastion 🧱 (Backend & Scripts Architect) / Crucible 🔥 (Test Architect) gates are role-owned, not risk-scaled).
- **F — Completion and release coherence: partial-to-strong as confirmation, none on specifics.** The human-owned delivery boundary — "Commit, push, PR, release, and archive follow ordinary repository policy and require their own explicit authorization" (S14); RDD approval "never commits, pushes, opens a PR, or overrides repository policy" (S6) — independently confirms Stage F's "Retain user-only merge and proven-safe cleanup" (plan, Stage F). `infer:` this is corroborating posture, not a new mechanism. On F's actual work — "live PR evidence", "Resolve history/completion/release sequencing without invented anchors", "Exercise the known DEBT-011 failure classes" (plan, Stage F; DEBT-011/012 remain open) — ODD/RDD has **no fit**: nothing in S1–S29 addresses release-artifact placement or known failure-class exercises.
- **G — Observed pilots: strong on epistemics, none on instrumentation.** "A number I publish and you cannot reproduce is a claim about my honesty" (S7) is the exact standard behind "distinguish walkthroughs from live results" and "Compare like-for-like" (plan, Stage G), and the negative example is in evidence: ODD's own benchmarks are vendor self-run, n=1 per cell, with the spec-by-reference cell reported as paraphrased "13 failing, still 23/26 after two feedback rounds" (S9, S21, S28) — directional only (research set, README gap 7). The plan's existing matched model — Isolated foundation-only validation: pinned HEAD exported twice as untouched baseline and candidate snapshot, "the same exact command in both snapshots, serialized", failure-set comparison (plan, Isolated foundation-only validation) — is precisely the reproducible design S7 demands. `infer:` ODD contributes the standard and the cautionary example but no instrument for "preparation effort, user decision rounds, correction rounds and escaped defects" (plan, Stage G); measurement remains the plan's own matched-comparison method. Stage G pilots remain pending — nothing here reports one.
